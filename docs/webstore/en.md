## Full description (plain text for store dashboards)

```text
AI Mode Exporter is an independent browser extension for Google Search AI Mode. Export the questions and answers you want to keep as clean Markdown and continue your conversation in Gemini or ChatGPT—or ask AI Mode directly from your browser toolbar.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Ask AI Mode from the toolbar — Click the extension icon, type a question, and open Google Search AI Mode in a new tab without leaving your current page. Press Enter to search or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter for a new line.
• Select only the turns you need — After AI Mode finishes answering, click "Continue in…" above the input bar (or "Continue this chat…" in the toolbar popup) to preview every question-and-answer pair, expand or collapse answers, and pick only the relevant turns.
• Clean Markdown export — Preserves headings, bullet and numbered lists, links, and tables in structured Markdown so your context stays readable.
• Continue in Gemini — Opens a new Gemini chat and attaches your selected conversation as a Markdown (.md) file in the draft composer.
• Continue in ChatGPT — Opens a new ChatGPT chat and inserts your selected conversation into the message composer (longer selections may appear as a text attachment).
• You review before sending — Nothing is sent automatically. Inspect the imported draft, add your follow-up prompt, and send when ready.
• Available in 9 languages — English, Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語, 한국어, Português (Brasil), 简体中文, and 繁體中文.

HOW TO USE

1. Open Google Search AI Mode (or click the AI Mode Exporter icon in your toolbar to ask a question).
2. Wait for AI Mode to finish generating its answer, then click "Continue in…" above the input bar.
3. Check the questions and answers you want to bring along and choose Gemini or ChatGPT under "Continue in".
4. Review the imported draft in the new tab, type your next prompt, and send.

GREAT FOR

• Turning a quick Google AI Mode search into a deeper brainstorming, writing, or coding session in ChatGPT or Gemini.
• Carrying over only the useful answers—without noisy off-topic turns.
• Saving structured tables, links, and step-by-step comparisons as portable Markdown context.

PRIVACY & PERMISSIONS

AI Mode Exporter does not collect personal data, track your browsing, or send your conversations to any third-party servers.

• Temporary local storage — Your selected export is stored locally on your device only during the handoff to Gemini or ChatGPT and is automatically cleared once imported or after 10 minutes.
• Site access (Google Search, Gemini, and ChatGPT) — Used only on Google Search to display "Continue in…" and read the turns you select, and on Gemini or ChatGPT to populate the new draft you open.

SUPPORT & LINKS

• Bug reports & source code: https://github.com/RonkTsang/ai-mode-exporter
• Email: ronktsang@gmail.com

AI Mode Exporter is an independent open-source extension by the maker of Gemini Power Kit and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google or OpenAI.
```
