# AI Mode Power Kit

Chrome Web Store listing draft.

## English

**Short description**

An independent browser extension for Google Search AI Mode. Continue selected questions and answers in Gemini.

**Full description**

AI Mode Power Kit is an independent browser extension for Google Search AI Mode. Use **Continue in Gemini** to carry the parts of an AI Mode conversation you want to build on into Gemini.

1. Choose the questions and answers you want to bring over.
2. Open Gemini with your selection attached to the draft as a Markdown file.
3. Add a follow-up prompt and send it when you're ready.

## 简体中文

**简短介绍**

适用于 Google 搜索 AI 模式的独立浏览器扩展。选取想保留的问答，然后在 Gemini 中继续。

**详细介绍**

AI Mode Power Kit 是一款适用于 Google 搜索 AI 模式的独立浏览器扩展。使用「在 Gemini 中继续」功能，把想进一步探讨的问答带到 Gemini。

1. 选取想带到 Gemini 的问答。
2. 打开 Gemini，所选问答会以 Markdown 文件的形式附到草稿中。
3. 输入后续问题，准备好后自行发送。
