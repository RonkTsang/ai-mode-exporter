---
title: Save a Google AI Mode conversation with Save as
description: Use AI Mode Exporter’s Save as menu to select questions and answers from Google AI Mode and export them as Markdown or PDF.
---

AI Mode Exporter lets you save an entire Google AI Mode conversation or just the question-and-answer turns you want to keep.

## Open the Save as menu

1. [Install AI Mode Exporter](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source) and open a conversation in [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50). Wait for the answer to finish.
2. Click **Continue in…** above the input. Check the turns you want to export, or choose **Select all**.
3. Open **Continue in** at the bottom right of the dialog, then open **Save as**.

![The Save as submenu in the AI Mode Exporter dialog shows Markdown and PDF options](/images/save-as.webp)

## Preview images and tables

The export includes tables and supported images from the selected answers, including image galleries. Before exporting, click **Show answer** to inspect the content of a turn.

![An expanded answer in the AI Mode Exporter dialog shows an image gallery above the answer text](/images/support-images.webp)

## Choose an export format

- **Markdown** downloads a `.md` file you can edit or import into a notes app. Follow the [Markdown export tutorial](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-as-markdown/).
- **PDF…** opens a local preview where you can review the document before printing or saving. Follow the [PDF export tutorial](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-as-pdf/).

To paste the selected turns into another app, choose **Copy** in the **Continue in** menu. This copies the Markdown text to your clipboard.
