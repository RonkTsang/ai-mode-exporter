---
title: How to continue a Google AI Mode conversation in Gemini
description: Transfer selected Google AI Mode questions and answers to a new Gemini draft as a Markdown attachment, then ask your follow-up.
---

Use AI Mode Exporter to bring selected questions and answers from Google Search AI Mode into a new Gemini draft as a Markdown attachment. For example, if AI Mode compared several train routes, you can bring over just the comparison and ask Gemini for a day-by-day itinerary.

## Transfer selected questions and answers to Gemini

1. [Install AI Mode Exporter in Chrome](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source), then open [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50) and let the answer finish.
2. Click **Continue in…** above the input. Select the questions and answers that Gemini will need, then choose **Gemini** from **Continue in**.
3. Check that a Markdown attachment appears in the new Gemini draft. Type a follow-up such as “Use the attached route comparison to plan a three-day trip by train.”
4. Review the draft and send it when ready.

![The Google AI Mode transfer dialog lets you select turns and choose Gemini or ChatGPT](/images/onboarding-selection.webp)

## If the Gemini attachment is missing

Sign in to Gemini if prompted, return to your original AI Mode tab, and start the transfer again. See [draft troubleshooting](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#the-destination-opens-with-an-empty-draft) if the attachment still does not appear.

You can also [continue the same AI Mode conversation in ChatGPT](/blog/continue-google-ai-mode-in-chatgpt/).
