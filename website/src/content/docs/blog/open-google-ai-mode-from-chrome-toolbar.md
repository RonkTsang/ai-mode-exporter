---
title: How to open Google AI Mode from the Chrome toolbar
description: Pin AI Mode Exporter in desktop Chrome and ask Google AI Mode a question from the toolbar. Your search opens in a new tab.
---

AI Mode Exporter lets you start a Google Search AI Mode conversation from the Chrome toolbar while keeping your current page open.

## Pin the extension and ask your first question

1. [Install AI Mode Exporter in desktop Chrome](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source).
2. Open Chrome’s **Extensions** menu in the toolbar and pin **AI Mode Exporter**.
3. Click the extension icon, type a question, and press **Enter** or click the search icon. Try “What can I do on a rainy weekend in Taipei without a car?”
4. Continue the conversation in the new Google AI Mode tab. For a different workflow, use **Continue in…** to [bring the relevant turns into Gemini](/blog/continue-google-ai-mode-in-gemini/) or [ChatGPT](/blog/continue-google-ai-mode-in-chatgpt/).

## If AI Mode does not load

Try opening [Google AI Mode directly](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50) in the same Chrome profile and follow any Google sign-in or verification prompts. If AI Mode opens directly but the toolbar search does not, [contact the developer](/support/).
