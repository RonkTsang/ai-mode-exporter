---
title: Quick start
description: Continue an AI Mode conversation in Gemini or ChatGPT.
---

Using the extension for the first time? [Install AI Mode Exporter from source](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source).

1. Open [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50), ask a question, and wait for the answer to finish.

   ![The continuation button above the AI Mode input](/images/onboarding-entry.png)

2. Click **Continue in…**, select the questions and answers you want, and choose **Continue in** followed by Gemini or ChatGPT.

   ![Selecting a question and choosing a destination](/images/onboarding-selection.png)

3. Review the imported draft in the new conversation, add your next question, and send it when you are ready.

   ![An imported conversation attached to a Gemini draft](/images/onboarding-gemini.png)

See the [step-by-step tutorials](/blog/) for Gemini, ChatGPT, and the Chrome toolbar. If the transfer stalls, see [how to troubleshoot an empty draft](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#the-destination-opens-with-an-empty-draft).
