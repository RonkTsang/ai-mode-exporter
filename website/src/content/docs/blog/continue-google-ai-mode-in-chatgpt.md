title: How to continue a Google AI Mode conversation in ChatGPT
description: Bring selected Google AI Mode questions and answers into a new ChatGPT draft in Chrome, then review the context and add a follow-up.
---

AI Mode Exporter brings selected questions and answers from Google Search AI Mode into a new ChatGPT draft. For example, carry over a product comparison and ask ChatGPT to turn your shortlist into a checklist.

## Transfer selected questions and answers to ChatGPT

1. [Install AI Mode Exporter in Chrome](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source), then open [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50) and let the answer finish.
2. Click **Continue in…** above the AI Mode input. Select the question-and-answer pairs you want and choose **ChatGPT** from **Continue in**.
3. Wait for the new ChatGPT draft to show the imported context. ChatGPT may turn a long selection into a text attachment.
4. Add your request, such as “Turn this comparison into a checklist and flag the details I still need to verify.” Review the draft before you send it.

## If the ChatGPT draft stays empty

Check that you can open a new ChatGPT chat. If you signed in or started typing before the import finished, go back to the original AI Mode tab and try a fresh transfer. For more help, see [draft troubleshooting](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#the-destination-opens-with-an-empty-draft).

If you prefer a Markdown attachment, see [how to continue in Gemini](/blog/continue-google-ai-mode-in-gemini/).
