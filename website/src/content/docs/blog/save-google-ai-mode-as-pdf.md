---
title: How to save Google AI Mode answers as a PDF
description: Export a Google AI Mode answer or conversation to PDF in Chrome. Preview the selected questions and answers, then save them in Chrome’s print dialog.
---

Use AI Mode Exporter to turn selected questions and answers from Google AI Mode into a PDF you can read offline or share.

## Open the PDF preview

1. [Install AI Mode Exporter](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source), open your conversation in [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50), and wait for the answer to finish.
2. Click **Continue in…** above the input. Check the turns you want to save, or choose **Select all** for the entire conversation.
3. Open **Continue in** at the bottom right and choose **Save as → PDF…**. The extension opens a local preview in a new tab.

![The Save as submenu shows PDF below Markdown](/images/save-as.webp)

## Save the preview as a PDF in Chrome

1. Review the questions and answers on the preview page. Click **Print / Save as PDF** in the upper right.

   ![The local AI Mode Exporter preview shows the selected conversation and the Print / Save as PDF button](/images/export-pdf.webp)

2. In Chrome’s print dialog, set **Destination** to **Save as PDF**. Review the page layout and adjust settings such as the page range or margins if needed.
3. Click **Save**, then choose a filename and folder.

For an editable file, see [how to export Google AI Mode answers as Markdown](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-as-markdown/). The [Save as guide](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-conversation/) covers both export options.
