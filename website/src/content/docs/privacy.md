Last updated November 28, 2026.

AI Mode Exporter is a browser extension developed by RonkTsang. It uses the content of your Google Search AI Mode conversation to let you preview, export, or continue it. The extension has no developer-operated backend and uses no analytics, advertising, or browsing-history tracking. We do not sell user data.

## Searches, previews, and exports

- **Toolbar search.** When you submit a question from the toolbar, the extension opens a Google Search AI Mode tab with your question in the URL. Google receives the question, and the URL may appear in your browser history.
- **Conversation preview.** The extension reads questions and answers from the AI Mode page, including links and images where available, so you can choose which turns to keep. It processes this content in your browser. When a preview displays a remote image, your browser may request it from Google or the image host.
- **Copy or save.** When you choose to copy or save a selection, the extension writes it to your clipboard or creates a Markdown file. If you use PDF export, the extension opens a local print preview and your browser handles printing or saving the file.

## Continuing in Gemini or ChatGPT

When you choose **Continue in**, the extension puts only the selected turns into a new draft on the service you choose. Gemini receives the selection as a Markdown attachment. ChatGPT receives it as draft text or, for longer selections, a text attachment. The destination may receive and process the draft or attachment as soon as it is inserted. You choose whether to send the message. Google and OpenAI handle data on their services under their respective privacy policies. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)

## Temporary storage and deletion

For a handoff, the extension temporarily saves your selection in local extension storage. It removes the record after a successful import. Unused records expire after 10 minutes and are cleared when the browser next starts, when another handoff starts, or when the expired record is accessed. PDF preview data is kept in browser session storage, cleared when the preview reads it, and expires after five minutes if unused. Expired PDF records are cleared on the next PDF export or when the browser session ends.

Removing the extension clears its local storage. You can delete saved files, clear your clipboard or browser history, and manage any imported drafts or chats on the destination service.

When you uninstall the extension, your browser may open an optional survey hosted on Google Forms. We receive your answers only if you submit the form.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

AI Mode Exporter handles user data in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. [Chrome Web Store Limited Use policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use)
