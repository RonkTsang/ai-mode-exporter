AI Mode Exporter helps you do three things in Google Search AI Mode: **continue** selected questions and answers in Gemini or ChatGPT, **save or copy** them as Markdown or PDF, and **start a new AI Mode search** from any tab using the Chrome toolbar.

:::note[Before you begin]
- **Install the extension** in desktop Chrome if you haven’t yet: [Install AI Mode Exporter from source](/guide/install-and-troubleshoot/#install-from-source).
- **Sign in ahead of time** to [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) if you plan to continue conversations there.
:::

## Continue a conversation in Gemini or ChatGPT

<Steps>

1. **Ask a question in Google AI Mode**

   Open [Google AI Mode](https://www.google.com/search?udm=50), ask a question, and wait for the answer to finish generating. Once it finishes, the **Continue in…** button appears above the input box.

   ![The Continue in button above the Google AI Mode input box](/images/onboarding-entry.webp)

2. **Choose the turns you want to bring**

   Click **Continue in…** to open the selection dialog. Check the questions and answers you want to keep (or click **Select all**), then open the **Continue in** menu at the bottom right and pick **Gemini** or **ChatGPT**.

   ![Selecting questions and answers and choosing Gemini or ChatGPT](/images/onboarding-selection.webp)

3. **Review the draft and send**

   A new conversation opens with your selected context already attached as a draft—nothing is sent automatically. Review the imported content, add your follow-up question, and send it when you are ready.

   ![An imported AI Mode conversation attached to a Gemini draft](/images/onboarding-gemini.webp)

</Steps>

:::tip[What to expect in each platform]
Gemini attaches your selected conversation as a Markdown file. ChatGPT inserts it directly into the message box (and may turn longer selections into a text attachment).
:::

## Save or copy a conversation

You don’t have to switch platforms to keep a useful answer. From the same **Continue in** menu at the bottom right of the selection dialog, you can also export locally:

- **Copy** — copies the selected turns to your clipboard as Markdown.
- [**Save as → Markdown**](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-as-markdown/) — downloads a `.md` file ready for your notes app.
- [**Save as → PDF…**](/blog/save-google-ai-mode-as-pdf/) — opens a clean local preview so you can print or save the conversation as a PDF.

![The Save as submenu showing Markdown and PDF options](/images/save-as.webp)

## Use the Chrome toolbar popup

Pinning the extension to your Chrome toolbar puts two handy shortcuts one click away.

1. Click Chrome’s **Extensions** icon (the puzzle piece) in the top-right corner of the toolbar.
2. Click the pin icon next to **AI Mode Exporter** so it stays visible on your toolbar.

### Ask AI Mode from any page

Click the pinned **AI Mode Exporter** icon on any webpage, type your question into **Ask AI Mode**, and press **Enter** (or click the search icon). Google AI Mode opens your search in a new tab while keeping your current page open.

![The pinned AI Mode Exporter icon opens a popup with an Ask AI Mode search field](/images/toolbar-popup.webp)

### Backup entry when the in-page button is missing

:::tip[Don’t see “Continue in…” on the page?]
Google occasionally updates the AI Mode page layout, which can temporarily hide the in-page button. You can always use the toolbar popup as a backup.
:::

While on a Google AI Mode tab with a finished answer, click the **AI Mode Exporter** icon in the toolbar and choose **Continue this chat…**. It opens the exact same selection dialog so you can continue in Gemini or ChatGPT, or save the conversation.

![On an AI Mode tab, the extension popup shows the Continue this chat option](/images/toolbar-popup-continue.webp)
